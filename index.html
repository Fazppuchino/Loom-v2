HELP & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to common questions about Lighten Learning and its student workspace.

1. What is Lighten Learning?

A concept for a calmer student workspace that brings assignments, grades, and planning tools together.

2. Who is this product for?

The sample concept is designed for students, families, teachers, and school teams.

3. What can I do in the student portal?

View assignments, check grades, and keep track of important dates from one organized workspace.

4. How do students use the demo?

Choose Student portal in the menu, then use the tabs to explore Overview, Assignments, Grades, and Calendar.

5. Can I create a student account?

Account creation is not available in this concept preview.

6. What can I see in Grades?

Review course averages and get a simple overview of academic progress.

7. Which schools are featured?

Oakridge Preparatory Academy, Maple Creek Charter School, and Harborview STEM Magnet.

8. What feedback is featured?

Students, teachers, and school communities share how a clear, approachable workspace can support learning.

9. How are assignments organized?

Assignments are grouped by due date and subject so it is easier to see what needs attention next.

10. Can I update assignment progress?

Yes. Check off completed assignments, and your progress will update across the Overview and Assignments panels.

11. What does the Grades panel show?

It displays course averages in a clear, easy-to-scan view.

12. What is shown in the Calendar?

See upcoming due dates and important learning activities in a weekly calendar view.

13. What is the focus timer?

A small browser-based timer with sample two-, five-, and ten-minute focus sessions.

14. Are quick tasks saved?

Quick tasks are temporary demo items and may reset when the page reloads.

15. Are notes saved?

The note tool attempts to save text in local browser storage on this device. Avoid entering sensitive information.

16. Does dark mode remember my choice?

The demo attempts to remember the appearance setting in this browser using local storage.

17. How does Lighten Learning handle personal information?

Notes and appearance preferences are stored in your browser when storage is available. Avoid adding sensitive information to notes.

18. How does Lighten Learning support student privacy?

Student privacy matters. Avoid sharing sensitive information in notes or through support contact channels.

19. Does it work on phones?

The layout adapts to smaller screens. Some screens may need scrolling on a phone to keep all controls accessible.

20. How can teachers support coursework?

Teachers can help students keep coursework organized by subject, due date, and progress.

21. How can families stay informed?

The Parents plan is designed to make upcoming work and learning progress easier for families to follow.

22. How can a school get started?

Contact our team to discuss your school’s needs and the right plan for your learning community.

23. Can Lighten Learning connect with school systems?

Contact our team to discuss your school’s technology needs and integration requirements.

24. What plans are available?

Choose from Class, School, District, and Parents plans. Visit Pricing to compare the options.

25. How do I ask about a plan?

Choose the plan that fits your needs and use its contact link to reach our team.

26. What is the product’s goal?

The fictional goal is to make school routines feel clearer, more approachable, and easier to plan.

27. How can I reach support?

Call (310) 555-0148 or email support@lighten-learning.help. You can also reach our team at team@lighten-learning.help.

28. How do I report a problem?

Email support@lighten-learning.help or call (310) 555-0148, and include a brief description of the issue.

ABOUT LIGHTEN LEARNING

Learning tools should make room to think.

We are building a calmer, clearer way for students and educators to stay on top of learning.

Our purpose

Bring everyday schoolwork into a clear view, make progress easy to notice, and keep the experience calm and welcoming.
